# Construction guarantees versus proof consequences

## Rule

A tool may record the incidence deliberately chosen by that operation and the conclusions of the earlier proposition it applies. Combining two separately constructed objects must not manufacture an unproved consequence. Non-metric orientation records describe the diagram; they do not themselves establish collinearity, length equality, or right angles.

## Changes

## Other operation records checked

Cut points and midpoints are deliberately placed on their input segment; angle-bisector and SSS endpoints are deliberately placed on the selected opposite segment or base ray. Perpendicular feet lie on their selected line; copied lengths on a selected directed ray and area bases on their selected ray are placements. Marked intersections record incidence only with their selected supports, and circle equality still requires recorded radius membership. Earlier equilateral, square, and area propositions supply their own certified conclusions; those guarantees do not supply additional cross-figure alignments.

Initial topology layouts were checked against their lesson's given figure: transversal intersections, stated common parallels, points on the given line, and the diagonal/complement configuration are input incidences. They do not assert new metric equalities. Compound proof deductions now also require the lesson's actual construction tasks to be complete.

## Saves and practice

Manual work is retained. Supplied proofs synchronize to the revised guide while retaining matching justifications. Incomplete or obsolete automatically supplied geometry is rebuilt without replacing the saved manual drawing. Old manually drawn E.44 figures using the inward auxiliary placement must be undone or restarted; hints identify the wrong-side point rather than asking for more points with already-used names.

## Checks

Integration tests exercise every construction and complete proof in Book I, automatic justifications, practice modes, moved display coordinates, and save/load. Added regressions remove each E.45 alignment/transitivity step, inject legacy alignment triples, attempt the former definition shortcuts, and remove E.47's alignments while trying equivalent Pythagorean statements. These shortcuts must fail.
